# Writing Landing Pages

## When to use

Use this skill when the founder needs landing page copy.

## Workflow

Write:

1. Hero headline
2. Subheadline
3. 3 benefits
4. Who it is for
5. Call to action
6. Short FAQ

## Style

- Clear
- Simple
- Not hype-heavy
- No fake statistics
- Focus on pain and outcome

## Output format

```markdown
## Landing Page Copy

### Headline

### Subheadline

### Benefits

### Who It Is For

### CTA

### FAQ
```
